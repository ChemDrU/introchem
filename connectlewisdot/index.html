How to build a Lewis structure

  1. Add atoms. Drag an element tile onto the workspace, or just tap/click one to drop it in.
  2. Arrange them. Drag any atom by its circle to reposition it.
  3. Form a bond. Click one unpaired (single) electron dot, then click an unpaired dot on a different atom. The two electrons merge into a shared pair between them.
  4. Double / triple bonds. Repeat the click-pair between the same two atoms using more of their unpaired dots.
  5. Break a bond. Click the shared pair sitting on the bond line — the electrons return home.
  6. Watch the badge under each atom: it fills in green once that atom has a full outer shell (8 electrons, or 2 for hydrogen/helium).
  7. Try a challenge from the sidebar, then press Check my structure once you think it's right.
